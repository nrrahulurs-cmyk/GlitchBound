# Quality work and evidence

The target is an exceptional voxel cyberpunk game across the whole experience: world design, surfaces, characters, animation, movement, vehicles, physical interactions, combat, sound and usability. **AAA parity has not been established.** A working demo, a favorable screenshot and passing tests are insufficient evidence. The public release is a playable foundation; this branch contains further local development.

Preserve the accepted direction: a large connected dystopian voxel city, Blender source assets, Three.js runtime, free exploration, varied supported advertisements, functional traffic, civilian and vehicle impacts, firearms with authored muzzle effects, no monoblade and no gun-impact dome.

## Review discipline

Use representative ordinary streets and service areas as well as the strongest boulevard. Retain camera coordinates, build hash, dates and failed findings. Compare equal-size images under comparable conditions where possible, plus uninterrupted walking/driving/combat clips and enabled audio. Official promotional stills can guide attributes but do not constitute matched performance or gameplay tests.

Do not ask a reviewer to favor this game. Preserve adverse judgments and state reviewer involvement. Anonymous left/right labels cannot make the visibly different art styles rigorously blind. A still-image preference cannot settle movement, physics, audio or interaction quality. Keep all major gaps visible until addressed and reviewed.

Keep the stable public release unchanged while the requested quality work is unfinished. A verified local iteration is a checkpoint, not evidence that the full quality target or the user's condition for another public deployment has been met.
