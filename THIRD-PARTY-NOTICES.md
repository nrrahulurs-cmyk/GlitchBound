- [Space Mono](https://github.com/googlefonts/spacemono): SIL Open Font License.

Fonts are requested from Google Fonts; system sans-serif, condensed, and monospace fallbacks are supplied when offline. Installed dependencies retain their own license files. Their licenses remain applicable independently of the project's source-code license.

The voxel models, carbine and projectile effects were authored for this project in Blender; audio is synthesized in code. The concept, landmark portrait and four initial advertising images were generated for the project. Generation prompts accompany the assets where provided.

The twelve additional campaigns—RECAST, KINKEEP, EVERON, QUIETUS, VYRA, GRINPOP, SYNTASK, NESTO, IRISYN, ELSE, VESPER ARC and SYNTHSILK—are generated voxel adaptations of user-supplied reference images. Their concepts and branding should not be described as wholly original project creations. `public/assets/user-advert-prompts.json` records their source references and restyling prompts.

Cyberpunk 2077 is referenced as visual inspiration. No game assets were extracted, and this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by its creators.
